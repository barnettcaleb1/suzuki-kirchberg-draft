1. WEIGHTED GRAPH FACTOR MAPS

The input is an actual GraphRelations.Family in an arbitrary complex unital
C*-algebra A : Type u. Vertices and edges are finite types in universe zero,
with decidable equality. The input vertex projections sum to 1; an edge w->v
satisfies s* s = p_v. Distinct initial cross-products vanish, and the outgoing
range sum is imposed at emitting vertices only. No nonzero-vertex hypothesis
or universal graph algebra is hidden in Family. Zero models are permitted.

The output matrices are CStarMatrix, with Mathlib's genuine operator norm on
the finite standard C*-module, not plain Matrix's elementwise norm. The finite
product factors have the maximum of their block C*-norms. The concrete corner
inherits the ambient norm and has unit q. Its inclusion into the ambient
matrix algebra is nonunital when q != 1. Local spectral-order instances do not
replace or alter the norm/topology. CommonCorner's closed-subalgebra and corner
instances and Mathlib's CStarMatrix definition were checked as support.

Coord = (Sigma v, Fin(k_v)) + (Sigma v, Fin(l_v)). The second-factor index is
(Sigma v, Fin(k_v)) + (Sigma e, Fin(l_source(e))). Its vertex block is indexed
by target(e), while its physical row is the source's Q slot. This exactly
matches the manuscript's M(v,w) = number of edges w->v convention.

The weighted operator's initial projection and orthogonality are proved from
the CK relations, including distinct-edge products whose physical rows agree.
Its contraction bound uses the C*-identity and the norm bound for an actual
projection. The resulting units satisfy multiplication and star relations.
matrixUnitHom extends them by finite matrix-basis induction, without assuming
a representation or universal property for the desired factor map.

factorHom (line 578) is a genuine UNITAL complex star homomorphism from the
incoming matrix-block product into the q corner. Its unit proof uses the CK
range sum and explicitly requires NoSinks source; its construction also uses
hk : forall v, 0 < k_v to choose the distinguished P coordinate.
firstFactorHom (line 690) is also a genuine unital complex star homomorphism,
but needs neither NoSinks nor positive k/l. The actual factor norm estimates
(lines 743/750) use automatic C*-hom contractivity.

The exact cardinalities are proved (lines 711-739): first block k_v+l_v;
second block k_v+sum_e [target(e)=v] l_source(e); for a matrix graph,
k_v+sum_w M(v,w)l_w. A non-symmetric test with M=[[0,5],[2,1]], k=[3,4],
l=[2,7] gives second dimensions 38 and 15, detecting transpose errors.

common_P, common_Q and common_Q_units are actual entry-level common-block
identities. recoveredEdge_eq and mixed_recovery require l_v>0 and prove the
displayed manuscript identities in this already concrete graph corner.
They do not construct CK generators in an abstract amalgam.

2. EVALUATION PATHS AND THE ACTUAL COMPLETED LIMIT

Channels stores actual C*-algebras and finitely many nonunital star maps with
orthogonal support units summing to 1. system constructs the total unital
step by summing these maps. The module does not take an opaque total step or
desired path as an input. One faithful channel is a sufficient condition for
step injectivity; it is not asserted necessary. A choice of a channel at every
level entails that the corresponding finite index types are nonempty.

path is actual iterated composition. path_compression (line 101) is proved by
induction, using a multiplication identity that composes support compressions.
final_compression allows a separately selected last noise branch. The support
is a proved self-adjoint idempotent. Membership in a supplied constant
StarSubalgebra follows from explicit one-step preservation hypotheses.
There is no separate exported pairwise-orthogonality theorem for all distinct
iterated paths; the proved compression identities suffice for the ideal route
used here. Positive summand/Cuntz-comparison arguments are not proved here.

evaluated_path_ne_zero (line 144) derives persistence from actual commuting
evaluation maps and injective evaluated maps at every step. Detection itself
is not automatic: the test with coordinate channels on C x C exhibits a final
orthogonal channel that annihilates the chosen path.

final_mem_ideal uses actual stage/transition compatibility and left ideal
multiplication in the norm-completed SequentialCStarLimit.Limit. extract_constant
(line 177) obtains nonzero d in K from injectivity of the noise map. It does not
claim the image of d is nonzero without further hypotheses; this distinction
is correct. The constant map is UNITAL. Pulling a closed ideal back to the
simple constant algebra then forces that ideal to be all of the target. No
injectivity of the constant map is needed for that argument.

isometry_of_stage_injective (line 220) is a genuine norm/completion argument.
It checks equality of norms on the dense algebraic direct limit and extends
over the completion using a closed equality set and continuity. Each algebraic
element is represented by a stage; norm_stage is the original stage norm.
The direct limit is completed in its stage norm. The unrelated raw set-quotient
topology is not substituted for the norm topology. Automatic C*-hom continuity
and the C*-isometry theorem for injective star maps supply the analytic steps.

Consequently exists_stage_kernel proves a noninjective unital star homomorphism
out of the completed limit kills a nonzero element already at a stage. The
independent probe builds the constant C x C system, extends first-coordinate
projection to its actual completed limit, and exhibits the nonzero stage
kernel element (0,1). This is a theorem about homomorphism kernels, not an
assumption that arbitrary dense unions meet arbitrary nonzero subspaces.

NoiseWitness (line 253) contains a finite path length/index, actual evaluation,
an injective noise map, a simple nonzero unital constant algebra, a unital
constant map, the literal factorization, and nonzero evaluation. Its Eval
algebra is not required finite-dimensional; this is a general theorem which
can later be instantiated by matrix evaluations. It does NOT store final
ideal fullness or limit simplicity as a field. ofEvaluatedPath derives final
detection from the specified initial detection/commutation/injectivity data.
An independent probe proves NoiseWitness for the zero input is impossible.

hom_injective_of_noise_witnesses (line 309) proves injectivity for every unital
map to every nontrivial C*-algebra, in an arbitrary target universe, from
detect : forall m a, a != 0 -> Nonempty (NoiseWitness choose m a).
The fixed global path choice is meaningful: the manuscript can use its
positive identity channel at each stage. The scheduled later occurrence of
each detecting representation and the actual graph/coefficient factorization
are still to be constructed. Supplying detect without that proof would assume
a new-contribution conclusion, not apply a licensed published theorem.

ClosedIdealKernelRealization (line 334) explicitly states that every proper
closed TwoSidedIdeal is the kernel of a unital map to a nontrivial C*-algebra
in the same carrier universe. This is mathematically the standard quotient
foundation, but it is neither proved here nor currently licensed. It is a
local hypothesis, not a global axiom. isSimple_of_hom_injective and
Channels.limit_isSimple are valid conditional deductions from it. Nontriviality
of the limit is proved from nontrivial stage zero and stage injectivity.

The result covers a homomorphism-kernel route to the product-limit simplicity
paragraph of manuscript Section 7. It does not yet instantiate even that
product's noise data. Nonsimple finite constant products require a different
blockwise fullness argument for the three constituent limits. No pure
infiniteness, nuclearity, or all-four-limits simplicity conclusion is proved.

  object A --corner--> L --(limitIso)^-1--> F --coefficient--> object B.
finalIso_unit (line 49) does not assume preservation of the final unit. It
derives it from exactly:
  unit A ; corner = projection ; stage n,
  projection ; coordinate n = prescribed ; R.j,
  prescribed ; coefficient = unit B.

R.j ; R.s = identity. Composition direction and the inverse orientation agree
with the manuscript. The independent test proves, using the first two
equations, that final unit preservation is equivalent to the third equation.

These are still local construction/interpretation hypotheses. projection is
an arbitrary categorical morphism in this statement, not an implemented
projection in the actual common algebra; its coordinate equation must come
from that actual projection. corner and coefficient must be the specified
full-corner and coefficient KK equivalences. object/scalar/unit need their
actual KK/K0 interpretation. No final unit equation is hidden, but the initial
class equations are not automatically available merely by naming them.
