Weighted bounds
===============
The generator bound is an arbitrary function bound : iota -> R, independent
of the representation index. No uniform global bound on all generators is
assumed. For every finite real free polynomial, induction constructs a finite
nonnegative bound: scalar constants use ||r|| and ||1||<=1; a generator uses
max(0,bound i); sums add bounds; products multiply bounds using norm
submultiplicativity. Thus even a family of individually unbounded generator
sizes is harmless, since each polynomial involves only finitely many operations.

The complex star algebra is the actual complexification C tensor_R FreeAlgebra
R iota. Tensor induction proves the weighted bound for complex evaluations.
Self-adjointness is required exactly where evaluation is bundled as a star
homomorphism. The bound statements themselves allow an empty representation
index; boundedFamily explicitly requires Nonempty J before forming the family
used by the supremum construction. Negative proposed bounds on an empty index
are vacuous, correctly handled by max(0,bound i); they do not enter the actual
amalgam family, whose index has a genuine zero model and whose bounds are norms.

Representation family, relations, and the actual norm
====================================================
Representation contains precisely a carrier in Type u, its actual CStarAlgebra
structure, two unital complex star homomorphisms from the fixed factors, and
equality of their composites with the fixed common-algebra maps. It contains
no universal property, faithful representation, generator separation, desired
amalgam, or claimed permanence property. Requiring compatibility in this model
index is the defining relation to be imposed; it is not assuming existence of
an amalgam satisfying the desired universal property.

The nonempty index is proved by actual homomorphisms to

unital C*-algebra with 0=1, not an inconsistent nontriviality instance. Every

Generators are all self-adjoint elements of the two factors, tagged by a sum.
Their bound is the original factor's fixed C*-norm. Each representation image
is bounded by that norm using Mathlib's automatic contractivity theorem for
actual complex C*-algebra star homomorphisms. This estimate does not presume
injectivity or an alternative norm on the factors.

The imported envelope constructs sup_R ||evaluation_R(a)||, proves a genuine
submultiplicative C*-seminorm, installs it on a separate Model type, takes its

Factor maps and universal property
=================================
An arbitrary factor element is represented as generator(realPart a) plus
i times generator(imaginaryPart a). The exact real/imaginary reconstruction
shows every compatible representation evaluates this expression to its given
factor image. Equation then proves zero, one, addition, multiplication,
complex scalar and star preservation in the quotient/completion. Therefore
left and right are actual unital complex star homomorphisms. Their commuting
equation follows from the actual model compatibility equation for every R.

For any given compatible pair into a C*-algebra B : Type u, the source bundles
that exact pair as one member of Representation and extends its evaluation
to the complete envelope using its proved supremum-norm contraction. No lift
existence is an additional assumption. lift_left and lift_right prove its
restrictions are the supplied maps.

Every free self-adjoint generator is proved to belong to the appropriate
factor image. FreeSelfAdjoint.hom_ext therefore gives equality on the polynomial
algebra from equality on the two factor maps. Density and continuity give
equality on the completion. These steps prove hom_ext and the actual exists-
unique clause in isFullAmalgam. No extraneous direct summand can evade this
uniqueness argument.

Universe scope is explicit: D, A0, A1 and the output are in Type u;
Representation is in Type (u+1); its targets are in Type u; and boundedFamily
has universe parameters {u,u,u+1}. The separated polynomial completion remains
in Type u. IsFullAmalgam.extension quantifies over all B : Type u, including
closed subalgebras of the output. A universe-polymorphic theorem extending to
arbitrarily larger target universes is not supplied. This is the documented
fixed-universe universal property, not a hidden restriction to matrix targets
or a selected class of separable representations.

Separability, faithfulness, and remaining boundaries
===================================================
The generic separability theorem assumes an actual IsFullAmalgam proof and
separability of both factors. It selects countable dense sets in the factors,
takes their two images, and closes their generated complex star algebra.
Continuity of the factor maps puts their whole ranges in this closed algebra.
The proved universal property forces this closed subalgebra to equal the whole
amalgam. The auxiliary separability result uses countably many finite words
and a separable scalar field C, not a false assertion that C is countable.
Closure is in the genuine norm topology. D need not be separately assumed
separable. constructedSeparable applies this result to the just-constructed
universal algebra and does not assume its desired separability.

left_injective and left_isometry require a supplied compatible pair f0,f1 into
an actual C*-algebra B and Function.Injective f0. The right-hand versions require
Function.Injective f1. The proof composes a proposed equality with the actual
lift and then uses that supplied faithful map. Thus the hypotheses refer to
a concrete external compatible representation and are visibly conditional;
they are not derivations from universality alone. Choosing B to be the output
and assuming its own factor map faithful would not discharge this obligation.
The present packet does not construct such faithful witnesses merely from
injectivity of the input maps i0,i1.

nontrivial requires a compatible pair into a supplied Nontrivial B. Its proof
uses unitality of the lift to rule out 1=0 in the output. No unconditional
nontriviality follows for arbitrary input maps; the explicit collapse example
confirms the expected boundary.
