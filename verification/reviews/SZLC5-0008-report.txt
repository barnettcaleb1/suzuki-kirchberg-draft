COMMON UNIT PROJECTION
stabilized is the actual block projection p direct-sum 1 in CStarMatrix indexed by Fin n plus Fin 1. A C*-matrix reindexing produces Fin (n+1). Nontrivial E, explicitly required for nonzero conclusions, makes the appended unit entry nonzero even when p = 0 or n = 0. The common algebra has two k-blocks of size n+1 and two l-blocks of size 1. Thus every block dimension is positive and k0+l0 = k1+l1.

commonProjection places the stabilized p and stabilized q, both as positive projections, in the two k-blocks, with zero in both l-blocks. The minus sign for the second K-class is not represented by a negative operator. The projection need not be the unit and need not be full in the initial common product. commonProjection_frame (123) requires an actual simple target D and an injective stage map, then derives nonzero image and a finite full-projection frame. The supporting SimpleFullness/FullProjectionFrame route uses the actual norm-closed ideal, Neumann inversion and C*-functional calculus; the frame is not assumed in this theorem.

stabilized_difference (136) is additive-group cancellation under an explicit direct-sum class law. It is not a definition or computation of K0. The InitialGraphPresentation supplement verifies the relevant integer signs, but a graph-K-theory identification is still required to use those signs for operators. The actual common projection and the frame implication are therefore substantial local steps, not the complete prescribed-unit argument.

COEFFICIENT MODEL CONDITIONAL
Suspension H is an actual closed nonunital star subalgebra of continuous H-valued functions on the compact interval, vanishing at both endpoints, with the inherited sup norm and separability. The inclusion f |-> (f,0) identifies it isometrically with the closed kernel of Cone(theta) -> I. shortExact (144) proves injectivity, exact range/kernel and surjectivity for any theta; injectivity of theta is not smuggled in.

The ramp section x |-> ((1-t) theta(x),x) is complex-linear and isometric, because its product norm is max(path norm, norm x) and theta is contractive. It is generally not multiplicative. The sqrtRamp factorization proves its matrix star-square identity, and actual positive C*-matrices are star squares by their C*-square roots. This establishes complete positivity at every finite matrix level, including empty index types, with CStarMatrix's operator norm. semisplit (278) proves a CPC right inverse rather than assuming it. The explicit StarOrderedRing hypotheses and canonical spectral order on the cone are used for the genuine C*-positive cone; the scalar norm estimates are not substituted for complete positivity.

quotientSuspensionMap (298) is a concrete star hom Cone(theta) -> Suspension Q for an actual quotient hom q : H -> Q satisfying q theta = 0. Its surjectivity theorem explicitly needs a bounded complex-linear right inverse sigma of q; the CPC variant constructs that bounded linear right inverse from an actual contractive CP section. It does not claim that an arbitrary surjective C*-hom has a bounded linear section.

For the manuscript, the original Dadarlat ideal embedding and semisplit extension, exact range(theta) = ker(q), the compact-path/C0 suspension correspondence, nuclearity of this constructed cone, and the standard mapping-cone KK/Bott identifications remain separate obligations. The module proves neither nuclearity nor a KK-equivalence. No UCT for the coefficient or target is assumed or concluded here. Its semisplit cone sequence should not be confused with the original Dadarlat extension needed for the KK triangle.

Common (184) gives the actual P-diagonal agreement and Q-diagonal outgoing sum. With strictly positive k,l and finite vertex/edge sets, edge = S_PQ T_QP has star(edge)*edge = p_target and outgoing range sum p_source, and distinct edges are orthogonal. The supported elements form a genuine family in pCp with inherited C*-norm, and graphLift uses the previously constructed fixed-universe GraphUniversal algebra. Scope caveat: Common imposes Q_diag at every vertex, including sinks. For a sink its right side is zero, so the associated vertex vanishes. This is stronger than the generic graph-family relation restricted to nonsinks. It is consistent with the intended manuscript graphs, which have positive outgoing adjacency, but this module should not be advertised as preserving nonzero vertices for arbitrary graphs with sinks. There is also no automatic faithfulness of graphLift for arbitrary S,T.

Kernel B is equivalent to Z with inverse z |-> (z,-z). Thus e0 has class +1 and e1 class -1, the kernel generator is (1,-1), and any balanced vector (a,a) has class zero.
