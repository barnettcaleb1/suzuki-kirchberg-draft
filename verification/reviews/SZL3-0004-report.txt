1. CornerFiniteness: the nonunital inclusion is handled correctly

The key algebraic input is an injective nonunital ring homomorphism f : R -> S. The function g(a)=f(a)+(1-f(1)) is bundled only as a unital multiplicative monoid homomorphism. It is not claimed additive or a star-algebra homomorphism. The identities f(a)f(1)=f(a), f(1)f(b)=f(b), and f(1)^2=f(1) make it multiplicative; additive cancellation preserves injectivity. Therefore Dedekind finiteness passes through g. Entrywise nonunital matrix amplification then passes ring-theoretic stable finiteness at every matrix size.

For the exact target finiteness convention, f is additionally star-preserving. The same complement map preserves star because f(1) is self-adjoint, so a proper isometry in R would produce one in S. The theorem stablyFinite transfers the precise statement forall n,v, v* v=1 implies vv*=1, using conjugate transpose on matrices, without a stronger ring-finiteness assumption. The specialized corner_stablyFinite_of therefore has exactly the desired target hypothesis. Separate corner_stablyFiniteRing/corner_stablyFinite statements with stronger ring hypotheses are also correct, but are not substituted for this theorem.

The concrete Corner hp comes from the norm-closed subtype pAp, with its inherited norm and its own unit p. Its ambient inclusion is genuinely nonunital. This follows from the inspected CommonCorner definitions, and compiled examples verify ||x in A||=||x|| and that the corner unit coerces to p. Separability is proved at the inherited metric norm topology; an explicit example gives the exported theorem that exact topology. Fullness and p!=0 are not necessary for stable finiteness or separability. The p=0 corner is correctly allowed to be the zero algebra.

2. CornerSimplicity: no abstract inheritance predicate is assumed

The ambient hypothesis is the exact closed-two-sided-ideal simplicity statement. Given a nonzero element x of a corner ideal, SimpleFullness.full_of_nonzero shows that the actual algebraic ideal generated by x is norm-dense in A. The supporting FullProjection.exists_sum_one is not a frame-existence axiom: it uses a norm approximation to 1 and the Neumann-series unit criterion to produce a finite exact expression sum a_i x b_i=1.

Compression turns this expression into the corner unit p. The identity compress(a*x*b)=compress(a)*x*compress(b) is valid because x has both p-support equations; compression is only a linear map in general and is never falsely assumed multiplicative. Each compressed summand belongs to the corner ideal, so the ideal contains the corner unit. This proves triviality of every algebraic two-sided corner ideal, even without assuming its closedness. That stronger conclusion is valid in this unital Banach setting because a dense ideal contains an invertible element.

The zero projection is handled correctly: ideals_trivial does not assert nontriviality. The theorem isSimple separately requires q!=0 and proves Nontrivial of the corner via its unit. It then supplies Target.IsSimple, whose definition includes nontriviality as well as the closed-ideal statement. No fullness premise or finite frame is silently added to this corner simplicity conclusion.

3. MultiplicityEmbeddings: actual matrix maps and exact dimensions

Blocks k is a finite product of complex full matrix C*-algebras, with the genuine matrix operator norms and finite-product norm. CopySlots and copyHom build a literal block-diagonal representation with one source block for each labelled copy. The map is complex-linear, star-preserving, multiplicative and unital. Its entry formulas exhibit copies and zero off-copy blocks. Injectivity follows when every source block appears. No positivity or K-theory map is supplied in place of an actual algebra homomorphism.

For M : Matrix target source N, targetSize k M(v)=sum_s M(v,s)*k(s), with target rows and source columns, exactly matching the manuscript convention. The number of actual slots is proved equal to this number. slotEquiv enumerates those coordinates, and rowHom/multiplicityHom are reindexed genuine star homomorphisms. The embedding theorem assumes every source column has a positive entry somewhere; strict positivity with a nonempty target is a sufficient specialization. Target block nonzero size is proved under a separate positive-row/nonzero-source-size hypothesis. Allowing zero sizes or empty indexing types does not assert nontriviality: those are legitimate zero-algebra cases, whereas the manuscript application supplies positive sizes.

The isometry theorem uses the actual C*-norm and injectivity. A compiled generic example checks its signature, and another identifies the finite product's topology with its metric topology. Lean drops unused target Fintype arguments from some purely algebraic definitions such as multiplicityHom and intoDimensions; the C*-norm/isometry results do retain finite target indexing. Thus arbitrary pointwise algebraic products are not silently endowed with a finite-product C*-norm by those definitions.

intoDimensions accepts the explicit equality targetSize k M=d. This is the necessary unital dimension equation, not a desired embedding or conjugacy premise. The first and second inclusion matrices are [I I] and [I A], and their target dimensions are proved k+l and k+A*l. The first map is injective; the second is injective under the no-zero-column hypothesis on A. Natural multiplicities are explicit. natCast_toNat_multiplicity proves exact recovery of every nonnegative integer entry, and strict positive integer multiplicities yield isometric embeddings after toNat conversion. Instantiating all integer dimension identities from a lifting theorem still requires their exact natural-number interfaces; no negative entry is accepted silently.

4. MultiplicityComposition and the exact scope of conjugacy

In MultiplicityEmbeddings, permutationUnitary is an actual unitary complex matrix, and reindex_eq_unitary_conjugation proves the exact conjugation formula. Equal finite counts of each source label produce a label-preserving bijection, then an equivalence of copy coordinates. copies_unitarily_conjugate_of_counts constructs a single unitary U that works for every source element a. It assumes equality of finite counts and coordinate enumerations, not a previously supplied unitary, abstract representation classification, or equality of the representations. Its scope is the constructed labelled-copy models; it does not classify arbitrary finite-dimensional star homomorphisms.

The separately frozen MultiplicityComposition closes the generic composite-map gap in the original file. CompositionCopies records an outer copy and an inner copy. compositionEnumeration explicitly reassociates their sigma coordinates and applies the two previously chosen enumerations. composition_normalForm proves equality of the entire reindexed copy representation with the composite homomorphism. Its case split covers distinct outer copies and distinct inner copies, so the theorem proves the off-copy zeros as well as the previously known diagonal-copy entries. It is stronger than composition_copy alone.

composition_copy_count gives a concrete fiber equivalence and proves that the source-label count is exactly (N*M)(w,s), with the correct target-row/source-column orientation. Equal natural matrix products therefore give equal counts. compositionDimensionEq transports the actual target dimension of the primed route to that of the fixed unprimed route. compositions_unitarily_conjugate then constructs, for each target row, one unitary that conjugates the two full composite maps simultaneously for every source element.

5. FiniteCPApproximation: actual complete positivity and contractivity

Target.HasCPApproximation quantifies over every finite subset s and every epsilon>0, with a positive matrix dimension n and two genuine complex completely positive linear maps, each contractive for every input, whose composite approximates each element of s in C*-norm. CompletelyPositiveMap in the pinned Mathlib library requires preservation of the spectral positive cone at every matrix size, and extends to arbitrary finite matrix index sets. It is not mere scalar positivity. The Target definitions and the relevant library class and nonunital-star-hom CP instance were inspected.

cpComp composes the actual CP maps at every matrix level. homCP uses the library's proof that actual star homomorphisms are CP. The unital positivity-reflection theorem proves self-adjointness by injectivity and then uses spectrum preservation under a unital injective C*-homomorphism. Thus no unproved order-embedding hypothesis replaces positivity reflection.

A full matrix algebra of positive dimension has the exact identity factorization. For finite products of complex matrix blocks, blockInclusion is the actual block-diagonal star homomorphism. blockCompression takes principal diagonal blocks and is a complex linear retraction. Its norm bound is proved using the supported block projection, the injective inclusion's norm isometry, the finite-product norm, and ||projection||<=1. It does not substitute an entrywise matrix norm or assume contractivity from positivity alone.

Complete positivity of blockCompression is proved by pinching: its amplified image under the injective block inclusion is a sum of D_i* M D_i, where D_i is a diagonal amplification of a block projection. Each summand is positive, and injective unital positivity reflection brings the result back to the actual matrix algebra over the block product. This holds at every matrix level, not only for scalar blocks. The positive-total-dimension factorization reindexes the total matrix space to Fin n and is exact on every input, with both norm bounds proved.

The final finiteProduct_hasCPApproximation also handles zero total dimension: then every block is empty and the product algebra is a subsingleton. CP zero maps through M_1(C) witness the target property while retaining n>0. Separate compiled examples instantiate both an empty product and a nonempty family of zero-size blocks. This is legitimate because the target does not require the approximating maps to be unital. No false nontriviality claim is made.

Actual star equivalences preserve CP approximation through isometric transport. Actual CP contractive retracts preserve it by composition and the error estimate for the retract. The contractivity and retraction hypotheses are explicit. The theorem concerns finite products of scalar complex matrix algebras. It does not classify arbitrary finite-dimensional C*-algebras or yet prove CP approximation for matrices over the coefficient E, spatial tensor products, or the constructed inductive limits. Equivalence of the target CP approximation definition with other manuscript formulations of nuclearity remains a separate interface/theorem obligation.

6. CornerCPApproximation: positivity reflection and a genuine CP retract

For injective nonunital star homomorphisms, positivity reflection is proved by the actual external unitization functor. Unitization.starMap is (lambda,a) |-> (lambda,f(a)), and its injectivity follows from injectivity of f; Unitization.inr_nonneg_iff identifies the original positive cone with the unitized one. These relevant Mathlib statements were inspected. No unitality of the original inclusion is assumed.

inclusionCP is the actual nonunital corner inclusion viewed as CP. compressionCP is a |-> p*a*p with codomain the genuine corner. At every matrix size, the included amplified compression is D* M D for D=diag(p). Positivity reflection for the injective amplified nonunital inclusion proves positivity in the corner matrix algebra itself. The norm estimate uses ||p||<=1, including p=0.

The approximation theorem composes an ambient CP approximation with inclusion on the input and compression on the output. The corner retract identity and compression's norm bound give the exact epsilon error without any assumed extension theorem. Compiled examples check inclusion(1_corner)=p, compression(1_A)=1_corner, the retract identity on every corner element, and complete positivity at an arbitrary natural matrix size.

isFiniteConstituent bundles separability, this exact CP approximation property, simplicity, and the precise target stable-finiteness predicate for any nonzero projection corner of an initial finite constituent. Nonzeroness is needed for the simplicity/nontriviality component; the CPAP theorem itself does not require it. No stronger ring-finiteness, additional trace, fullness, or nuclearity-classification hypothesis is introduced.

The projection images in A0 and A1 are nonzero by injectivity, so all three corner constituents satisfy the inherited target properties. The equality of the two images in P is obtained from the supplied commuting equation, in the orientation required by the second restricted factor map.
