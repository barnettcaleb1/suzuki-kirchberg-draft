1. Concrete hereditary closures and support equations

The source algebra is a genuine complete unital complex C*-algebra and p is an actual star projection. Corner hp is the norm-closed subtype pAp from CommonCorner, with its inherited norm and internal unit p; its ambient inclusion is nonunital. No alternate norm, quotient topology, or opaque hereditary predicate is introduced.

right_support_of_square proves v*p=v from (v* v)*p=v* v by showing (v(1-p))*v(1-p)=0 and invoking the C*-identity's zero-square criterion. left_support_of_square applies this to star(v) and takes adjoints. Thus the partial-isometry witness is not merely assumed supported.

For b in pAp, the range of a |-> b*a*b in A is contained in the closed corner. closure_minimal proves the same for the actual ambient norm closure. This containment needs neither positivity of b nor an assumed hereditary-subalgebra theorem. Continuous compression is a |-> p*a*p into the subtype. The identity compress(b*a*b)=b*compress(a)*b uses the two support equations for b, and yields a map from the ambient sandwich range into the internal sandwich range. Continuity passes this map to closures. Conversely, the isometric inclusion maps the internal range and its closure into the ambient range closure. Together with compress(x)=x on the corner, mem_hereditary_iff proves exactly the correspondence of these two norm closures.

These closures match Target.hereditarySet verbatim: closure(range(x |-> b*x*b)). An explicit Lean example instantiates mem_hereditary_iff with the bundled target's hereditarySet. Additional examples identify the corner topology with its metric topology and verify continuous_compress with explicit metric topologies on both source and target. There is no unsupported passage between weak/operator/quotient and norm closures. Identifying this target convention with every other formulation of a generated hereditary C*-subalgebra is not a theorem added by this file; the present inheritance theorem acts directly on the stated target convention.

2. Infinite projections, order instances, and exact Kirchberg inheritance

Target.IsInfiniteProjection requires an actual projection r and an actual element v with v* v=r, q=vv* a projection, q<=r, and q!=r. Starting with r in pAp, projection order implies r<=p. Hence q<=p. The proved support lemmas yield p*v=v=v*p, so v itself belongs to pAp. The original equations and nonzero defect remain valid under the injective subtype inclusion. The inequality q<=r is transferred into the corner using positivity reflection for the injective nonunital corner inclusion, rather than by assuming a subtype order is definitionally the desired C*-order.

The local instance pinning in isInfiniteProjection and isPurelyInfinite is meaningful. A separate synthesis probe outside those local declarations returns Subtype.instLE and the subtype preorder, while PartialOrder and StarOrderedRing are the corner's spectral instances. The two proofs explicitly pin PartialOrder, StarOrderedRing, Preorder and LE to the spectral order used in the bundled target. A successful expanded example verifies that the exported infinite-projection theorem supplies its inequality using (CStarAlgebra.spectralOrder (Corner hp)).toLE. Therefore the existing final statements do concern the target C*-order despite this raw-subtype instance ambiguity. Future generic order statements on the raw corner type should likewise control their order instances; no false theorem or required source repair is identified here.

For each positive nonzero b in the corner, inclusion preserves positivity and nonzeroness. Ambient pure infiniteness provides an infinite projection r in the ambient hereditary norm closure. The preceding containment places r in pAp, the closure equivalence places it in the internal hereditary set, and the witness restriction proves its infiniteness there. No Morita-invariance or abstract pure-infiniteness inheritance premise is assumed.

isPurelyInfinite allows p=0, in which case the quantified nonzero positive input does not exist; this is the intended vacuous zero-corner case. isKirchberg separately requires p!=0. 6. CPApproximationLimits: exactly what is assumed and exactly what is derived

The only additional structure is CPCSplitting S with three fields: an actual CP map retract_n from stage n+1 to stage n; a pointwise norm contraction bound on that map; and the equality retract_n(step_n(a))=a. No compatibility of nonadjacent retractions, assumed completed-limit map, convergence, finite-set approximation, or limit CPAP is a field. The independent #print of the structure and the final theorem's full signature confirm this.

Step injectivity is derived from the left inverse. To retract toward fixed stage k, stageMap k m uses the forward star-homomorphic transition when m<=k and successively composes adjacent CP retractions when m>k. Its contraction bound is proved inductively. stageMap_step follows from the directed-system law below k and the left-inverse equality above k. stageMap_transition derives compatibility for every m<=n by induction. LimitCPMaps then extends this now-proved compatible family to an actual CPC map limitRetraction k on the constructed completion.

The map limitRetraction k is a genuine left inverse on stage k. Inclusion after retraction fixes every stage m<=k. For an arbitrary limit point x and a stage-m approximation a with m<=k, the proof derives
  norm(stage_k(limitRetraction_k(x)) - x) <= 2 * norm(x - stage_m(a)).
The estimate uses contractivity and actual stage isometry. For each finite set s and epsilon>0, density gives an epsilon/2 approximation to each element. The maximum of finitely many stage indices supplies N such that the retraction error is strictly less than epsilon for all k>=N and all x in s. Empty s is handled by the empty finite supremum. Pointwise convergence then follows from the singleton case; it is not assumed.

Given exact Target.HasCPApproximation at each stage, the proof chooses a late retraction approximating the finite input set within epsilon/2, applies stage CPAP to its finite image within epsilon/2, and composes the two CP maps with the retraction and stage inclusion. Both composite maps are actual contractions, the intermediate matrix size remains strictly positive, and the norm triangle inequality yields the requested strict epsilon bound. This matches Target.HasCPApproximation's quantifiers over every finite set and every positive real epsilon, with both maps completely positive at all natural matrix sizes.

The final splitLimitAlgebra and splitLimit_hasCPApproximation explicitly omit a separate injectivity Fact and derive it locally from CPCSplitting. The independent explicit example has only S, R:CPCSplitting S and stage CPAP as inputs, and its conclusion is precisely Target.HasCPApproximation R.splitLimitAlgebra. There is no unproved colimit-existence premise. Universe u is arbitrary for the sequence's stage carrier types; this is a sequential, unital construction. Intermediate statements retaining the derived Fact are interfaces to the existing concrete limit construction, not additional logical assumptions in the final formulation.

The theorem is conditional preservation for systems equipped with actual adjacent CPC left inverses. It is not a proof that every injective system has such splittings or a formal proof of general nuclearity permanence under inductive limits. MatrixRetractions provides a genuine special case, but the necessary splittings for all manuscript channels remain unconstructed.

7. SuzukiFiniteDiagram: faithful integer conversion and both actual finite squares

The row/column conventions match manuscript Section 3: multiplicities act on column vectors, with target blocks as rows. B=A-I and B'=A'-I are strictly entrywise positive integer matrices. The source formulas are exactly
  T = [[S-Z, S-H-Z(1+B)], [Z, H+Z(1+B)]],  R = S+B'Z,
and the two equations are [I I]T=S[I I] and [I (1+B')]T=R[I (1+B)]. MatrixDiagrams' supporting declarations supply these literal identities, the second under SB=B'H.

natMatrix applies Int.toNat, which would truncate negative entries if used carelessly. Every product conversion here requires explicit nonnegativity of all four factors. natMatrix_cast proves the cast back to integers is exactly the original matrix. natMatrix_product_eq maps products through Nat.castRingHom and uses cast injectivity. Thus neither product equality is obtained by silently discarding signs. The integer theorem supplies strict positivity of S,T,R, and positivity of B and B' ensures nonnegative (indeed positive) I+B and I+B'.

HasMultiplicity is not an opaque predicate for a hoped-for K-theory map. It explicitly supplies a coordinate equivalence from the labelled copy slots to each target Fin(d_w), an actual target unitary, and equality of the given map with the conjugated block-copy representation on every source element. Its dimensions theorem derives the exact unital equation targetSize(k,M)=d by cardinalities of those coordinate equivalences. intoDimensions has this certificate with unitary 1.

exact_square_with_multiplicity constructs the corrected final factor map from compositions_unitarily_conjugate. That supporting theorem explicitly flattens copies, counts labels by natural matrix multiplication and matches equal label fibres; its hypothesis is equality of multiplicity products, not assumed conjugacy or a classification theorem. The correction composes the row representation with a coordinate cast and inverse target unitary conjugation. Injectivity is proved from nonzero columns of the final multiplicity matrix, so the corrected map is a C*-isometry. Its new certificate uses the composite coordinate equivalence and the inverse unitary. Conjugating therefore has not lost the requested multiplicities. exact_square_intoDimensions transports only through proved dimension equalities.

commonMap k l T is fixed as the standard actual unital embedding into common target block sizes obtained by splitting T*(k,l) into targetLeft and targetRight. natural_realization applies the exact-square construction twice with precisely this same T/commonMap. The two independent corrections are on the two factor edges, leaving their common edge shared. Realization contains the actual left/right unital star homomorphisms, explicit T/S/R multiplicity certificates, seven isometries (three vertical maps and four common-to-factor maps), and both literal equalities of star homomorphism compositions. The general natural realization requires nonzero columns for A,A',T,S,R; arbitrary zero source block sizes remain allowed at that interface. integer_realization obtains these nonzero columns from the positive integer data and an explicit Nonempty target index. The final positive-dimension theorem separately requires a nonempty source and positive k and l. In the Fin form, hr:0<r and hr':2<=r' supply exactly those nondegeneracy hypotheses. No compatible positive lift is assumed at this final entry point.

This now proves the manuscript's finite channelwise two-square realization from positive lifts. It does not yet prove the multiple-channel construction in the last paragraph of manuscript Section 3: each channel's target common dimensions are T_c*(k,l), whereas that paragraph uses sum_c T_c, disjoint coordinate inclusions and orthogonal projections summing to 1.
