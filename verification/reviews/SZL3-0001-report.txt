1. Completion and the concrete directed limit

CStarCompletion assumes an actual normed complex star algebra satisfying the C*-identity: NormedRing, StarRing, CStarRing, NormedAlgebra C, and (for the CStarAlgebra conclusion) StarModule C. Completeness of the source is not assumed. The ordinary uniform/metric completion is equipped with star by Completion.map; dense induction proves the star-ring identities, scalar compatibility, and C*-identity. The resulting type has an actual complete CStarAlgebra instance. The inclusion is an isometric unital star homomorphism with dense range.

The completion lift requires an explicit contractive bound on a source star homomorphism. This is a sound requirement for the possibly incomplete source; the code does not assume continuity of every algebraic star homomorphism from an incomplete C*-normed algebra. The directed-limit construction supplies this bound from contractivity of the stage maps into the complete target. Uniqueness follows from density and continuity.

DirectedCStarLimit takes a nonempty directed preorder I : Type v, CStarAlgebra stages A i : Type u, a coherent system of actual unital complex star homomorphisms, and injectivity of every transition. It builds the algebraic direct-limit quotient and defines its norm by the norm of a representative. Injectivity of the transition maps implies isometry, making this definition independent of representatives. Common-stage induction establishes a genuine RingNorm, not a seminorm with undischarged separation. The normed-algebra and CStarRing structures are constructed. Its Limit is then the concrete metric completion, in Type (max u v).

The stage maps into this completion are isometric, commute with the transitions, and have dense union. Compatible unital star homomorphisms to an arbitrary complete C*-algebra are extended and are unique. There is no assumed existence of a completed limit, no assumed dense cocone, and no assumed colimit universal property in these construction hypotheses.

The norm/quotient topology issue was checked explicitly. At DirectedCStarLimit.lean:111 a local TopologicalSpace instance takes the topology of the constructed metric. This is used for algebraicSeparable. Successful Lean examples identify the exported algebraicSeparable proposition with that metric topology, and identify limitSeparable with the completion metric topology. Thus the countable-stage separability results actually concern C*-norm topology.

Nonblocking API caveat: outside that local instance, a separate successful #synth probe returns instTopologicalSpaceQuotient for TopologicalSpace (Algebraic A f), while UniformSpace resolves through the metric. The raw type is a quotient, so downstream code that independently infers only a TopologicalSpace can choose the raw quotient topology. The current audited theorems do not confuse these structures. Future generic topological statements on Algebraic should explicitly install the norm topology (or use a wrapper with controlled instances). This is a maintenance hazard, not a demonstrated false conclusion in this version.

2. Sequential limits and full amalgams

SequentialCStarLimit composes adjacent maps along N, proves the directed-system laws and injectivity from injectivity of the adjacent steps, and instantiates the preceding concrete construction. The full printed signature of isSequentialColimit has only a System and the Fact that every step is injective; a colimit-existence premise is absent. The resulting object stays in the stage universe u because the index is N. The generic directed lift can target other universes; the packaged IsSequentialColimit and IsFullAmalgam predicates used here explicitly quantify over C*-algebras in their prescribed universe. This is the stated category-level universe convention, not quantification over one hand-picked target.

Natural transformations induce actual unital star homomorphisms on limits. Pointwise injectivity implies isometry and injectivity of the induced map, using equality of norms on the dense stage union. Countable-stage separability is retained.

The new isFullAmalgam theorem assumes four injective sequential systems, natural maps between them, and the full-amalgam property at each stage. Supporting InductiveAmalgam proves preservation from supplied colimit universal properties; the new theorem supplies those properties using the concrete construction. Thus the formerly conditional colimit input is discharged here. Existence and injectivity of the intended stage diagrams remain inputs: this is not a construction of the graph/amalgam stages, nor a proof that arbitrary candidate diagrams satisfy the stage universal property. The conclusion is a full universal property for the limit cocone; turning it into an identification with a separately constructed named full-product object requires that object's corresponding universal property, not a reduced-product replacement.

3. Actual finite-dimensional representations and tail separation

ResidualRepresentations.Representation A consists of a positive natural matrix dimension and an actual unital complex star homomorphism A -> CStarMatrix (Fin dimension) (Fin dimension) C. IsRFD means every nonzero element is detected by one such representation. SeparatingTails quantifies as: for every N and every nonzero a, there exists n >= N for which rho n detects a. These are concrete finite-dimensional star representations, not arbitrary maps or K-theory data.

For a nontrivial separable unital C*-algebra, open detection sets and hereditary Lindelofness give a countable separating sequence. Nontriviality is explicit: it supplies a representation by detecting 1 and hence a nonempty indexing type. Repetition via Nat.unpair makes every tail separating, with Nat.pair j N witnessing the lower bound. Equality detection is obtained by applying this statement to a-b. No homotopy of the representations is assumed. IsRFD also pulls back along injective unital star homomorphisms.

The RFD definition is element-separating. A separately imported model theorem using a different definition, a product embedding, or arbitrary nonunital representations would still need its interface bridge to this exact definition. The present manuscript asks for the actual unital separating family, so this formulation matches that step. It does not itself prove the manuscript's further spatial-tensor separation by {id_X tensor rho_n} for every X.

4. Stable finiteness without a hidden trace assumption

ResidualRepresentations.isStablyFiniteRing proves the stronger ring statement at every finite matrix size: xy=1 implies yx=1. If an entry of yx-1 were nonzero, an actual finite matrix representation detecting it would contradict finite matrix-ring finiteness. The conversion to the target convention, star(v)*v=1 implies v*star(v)=1 at every matrix size, is then immediate. No faithfulness or existence of a limit trace is assumed.

LimitFiniteness.of_dense_pairs assumes actual complete C*-algebra stages, injective unital stage embeddings into a C*-algebra B, density of simultaneous pairs from a single stage, and ring-theoretic Dedekind finiteness of every stage. For xy=1, simultaneous close approximation provides a,b in one stage with ||1-ab||<1 and ||1-x e(b)||<1. Completeness and the Neumann-series criterion make ab invertible in the stage. Stage Dedekind finiteness makes b a unit. Thus e(b) and x e(b) are units, so x is a unit and yx=1. This is an actual Banach/C*-norm argument. Pair density, rather than just unrelated single-element density, is proved for the constructed sequential limit using a common stage in the algebraic limit.

MatrixLimits implements actual entrywise star-homomorphism amplification, injectivity, and simultaneous density of two matrices over a common stage. A finite family of entries is moved to the maximum of its representative indices. This handles empty finite indexing types without adding a nonempty assumption.

The matrix topology is the genuine operator C*-norm topology. In pinned Mathlib Analysis/CStarAlgebra/CStarMatrix.lean, the norm is ||toCLM M||, and uniformity_eq_aux proves agreement with the finite product uniformity before NormedAddCommGroup.ofCoreReplaceAll installs the normed structure. The matrix-density proof uses this genuine equality with the finite entry topology. Two successful explicit Lean examples show that CStarMatrix's inferred topology equals both its metric topology and Matrix's finite product topology. There is no substitution of an entrywise maximum norm as a C*-norm.

Finiteness is transferred at every matrix size using the actual CStarMatrix algebras, and the ring equivalence with plain Matrix gives IsStablyFiniteRing of the limit. The printed stablyFinite_of_rfd signature assumes exactly an injective sequential system and IsRFD of each stage; its conclusion is StableFiniteness.IsStablyFinite of the constructed limit. The limit is not claimed to be RFD. No simplicity, trace, nuclearity, or tensor-product hypothesis is hidden in this theorem. Using ring-theoretic stage finiteness is stronger than merely restating the target isometry convention, but RFD proves that stronger premise here, so this does not leave a gap for the claimed RFD application.

5. External unitization and RFD amplification supplements

UnitizationRFD starts with an actual NonUnitalCStarAlgebra F and actual nonunital complex star homomorphisms to positive-dimensional complex matrix algebras. The external unitization is Mathlib's Unitization C F. The scalar quotient is an actual unital star homomorphism, and the 1-by-1 scalar representation detects every element whose scalar coordinate is nonzero. For scalar coordinate zero, nonzero unitized elements have a nonzero F coordinate, which is detected by a given F representation and its actual Unitization.starLift extension. The latter is lambda*1+rho(a). This argument remains valid when F was already unital or F is the zero algebra; the external scalar coordinate is preserved.

Separability uses the genuine norm-topological uniform equivalence between Unitization and C x F. Mathlib's normed-unitization uniformity construction and C*-identity were inspected. An explicit Lean example verifies the exported separable theorem at the unitization metric topology. UnitizationRFD.exists_separating_tails therefore supplies exactly the manuscript's unital tail-separating representations of E, conditional on actual separability and IsRFD of F. The construction of F and its nuclearity/KK-equivalence are not assumed to have been proved by this bridge.

RFDAmplification.flatten is an actual complex star-algebra equivalence from matrices of complex matrices to a matrix indexed by the product. The representation has the exact dimension n*dimension(rho), positive when n>0, and combines entrywise amplification, flattening, and finProdFinEquiv reindexing. The matrix_rfd proof detects a nonzero entry and uses injectivity of the equivalence. For n=0 there is no nonzero matrix to detect, which is the correct vacuous RFD case. A compiled auxiliary example confirms flatten is isometric for the actual C*-norms.

The finite_product_rfd theorem composes a coordinate evaluation star homomorphism with a detecting representation of that coordinate. Finite products carry their actual C*-algebra structure. Empty products again have no nonzero element and cause no false nontriviality assertion. For the manuscript's finite sums of matrix algebras over E, these are the needed RFD closure operations; identifying any differently packaged finite-direct-sum or tensor-with-finite-matrix stage with these concrete types remains an interface obligation.
