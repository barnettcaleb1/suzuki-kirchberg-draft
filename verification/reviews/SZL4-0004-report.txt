Separation, norm, and completion
1. CStarSeminorm uses the actual metric SeparationQuotient, not an arbitrary
   ideal quotient predicate. Its star descends because the norm-isometric star
   is continuous and hence preserves inseparability. Ring operations and norm
   are the pinned Mathlib quotient operations and induced norm.
2. The only Cstar assumption at this general input stage is the explicit Fact
   forall a, norm(a)^2 <= norm(star(a)*a), together with actual seminormed-ring,
   normed-star and complex-algebra structures. It is not a completion or
   universal-property premise. Quotient CstarRing is proved by representatives.
   Envelope is Completion (SeparationQuotient A), in the same carrier universe.
3. CStarCompletion extends star with Completion.map of the actual isometry;
   involutivity, anti-multiplication, addition, complex scalar compatibility,
   and the Cstar inequality follow by closed-set/dense-range induction. The
   resulting completion uses its genuine complete normed-ring structure.
4. A given contractive star representation is descended using continuity and
   separation of its Cstar target, then extended by the actual uniform
   Completion.extension. The extension's algebraic operations are proved by
   density. Uniqueness uses automatic continuity only for actual homomorphisms
   whose domain and codomain are complete Cstar algebras. No unjustified
   automatic-continuity claim for the incomplete input algebra is used.
5. Independent EnvelopeProbe proves equality of quotient/inclusion images iff
   the seminorm of their difference is zero; the full Cstar norm equality;
   dense inclusion; and an explicit ExistsUnique extension statement.

Supremum family
6. BoundedFamily has actual star homomorphisms into actual Cstar targets, a
   Nonempty index, and a pointwise BddAbove range of representation norms for
   every algebra element. This is pointwise polynomial boundedness, not one
   uniform bound for the entire algebra and not an assumed universal norm.
7. Subadditivity, submultiplicativity, scalar bound and star isometry are proved
   using these finite suprema. The Cstar lower inequality is proved using a
   square-root bound on every representation norm, with explicit nonnegativity.
   The independent probe recovers the full square equality for the supremum.

   representation into an unrelated target universe z.

   kernels. Injectivity needs the explicit separation hypothesis; it is not
   smuggled into the construction. UniversalCStarRelations proves exactly

Free self-adjoint algebra
11. FreeSelfAdjoint.Algebra is literally Complex tensor[Real] FreeAlgebra Real iota.
    Pinned Mathlib reverses free words and fixes real coefficients; tensor star
    conjugates the complex factor. Its complex StarModule law is proved.
12. Evaluation exists for any self-adjoint generator family in an actual Cstar
    algebra, with no contractivity premise. Star preservation follows by free
    algebra and tensor induction. Hom extensionality follows from the two
    algebraic universal properties. The audit proves the corresponding exact
    ExistsUnique property, conjugation of complex scalar multiples, and that
    free generators are nonzero by evaluation into Complex.
13. Contractive generators separately imply uniform bounds over the given
    family of targets for each real polynomial, then each tensor polynomial.

    BoundedFamily.bounded, rather than postulated. FreeProbe also proves the
    completed generators are contractions and determine completed homomorphisms.
