MatrixCPApproximation: mathematical correspondence
=================================================
1. Norm and order. The carrier is Mathlib's CStarMatrix over an actual complex
   CStarAlgebra. Its norm is definitionally the continuous-operator norm of
   CStarMatrix.toCLM; this was independently checked by rfl. No entrywise norm
   instance or substitute matrix norm is installed. norm_le_sum_entries proves
   only an upper estimate for this genuine norm, using the operator bound and
   finite Hilbert-C*-module vector bounds. The finite product carries the
   genuine finite Pi norm. StarOrderedRing constrains the positive cone by
   sums of star squares, rather than permitting an arbitrary convenient order.
   The final bundled coefficient/product results use spectral order explicitly.

2. Complete positivity is at every matrix level. amplify uses the actual
   flattening star-algebra equivalence for nested matrices, applies the input
   CP property to the flattened positive matrix, and reflects it back. It does
   not replace complete positivity by positivity at level one. flatten is an
   algebraic star equivalence over the coefficient algebra; its later norm
   preservation follows from the genuine C*-algebra theorem for injective star
   homomorphisms.

3. Complete contractivity is proved. The explicit positive Gram matrix is
   [[a* a, a*], [a, 1]] = X*X. Its CP image also proves adjoint preservation.
   Under the exact subunital assumption phi(1) <= 1, adding the nonnegative
   lower-right correction 1-phi(1) and compressing by [[1,0],[-phi(a),0]] gives
   phi(a)*phi(a) <= phi(a*a). contractive_of_norm_one requires only
   ||phi(1)|| <= 1, uses positivity to derive subunitality, and then uses the
   C*-identity and the positive-linear-map bound on a*a. amplify_one identifies
   the amplified unit image as the actual diagonal of phi(1), proving the
   amplification contractive from the original contraction hypothesis.

4. Quantifiers and universe scope. General amplification is provided for
   finite index types in arbitrary universes. amplify_contractive's index k
   is Type (universe zero), enough for every Fin n used in target CPAP. The
   final finiteProduct_hasCPApproximation likewise has block index iota : Type,
   while the coefficient algebra can lie in an arbitrary universe. No
   nontriviality or nonemptiness assumption is silently added to the finite
   product theorem. It assumes Target.HasCPApproximation of the coefficient.

5. Approximation tolerances and degenerate cases. The positive-size matrix
   proof approximates the finite set of all entries at epsilon/(n*n+1), uses
   actual CPC amplifications, and flattens M_n(M_m(C)) to M_(n*m)(C) through
   an isometric star equivalence. The output matrix size remains strictly
   positive and the final error is strictly less than epsilon. For n=0 the
   source is subsingleton and the separate existing CPAP theorem applies.

6. Finite blocks. blockInclusion is the concrete block-diagonal unital star
   homomorphism; blockCompression extracts the actual principal diagonal
   blocks and is its exact left inverse. The inclusion is injective and thus
   isometric. A single supported block equals compression q_i M q_i by a
   genuine projection. This proves norm contraction. At each matrix level,
   blockCompressionCP is positive because its injective amplified inclusion
   is the sum of the diagonal-projection conjugations of the input. Positivity
   reflection is proved for an injective star homomorphism. This yields an
   actual CPC retraction and target CPAP for the finite product. Empty products
   and all-zero block sizes are legitimate zero algebras, not contradictory
   unitality assumptions.

OrthogonalFiniteDiagrams: mathematical correspondence
====================================================
1. Actual coordinate embeddings. Family w is the finite product of Blocks
   (w c), with independently varying source weights for each channel. stack
   is concrete block diagonal inclusion. factorStack uses explicit finite
   coordinate equivalences to swap channel/copy indices and align the result
   with the standard target multiplicity homomorphism. factorStack_square
   proves entrywise equality, rather than assuming conjugacy or a commuting
   square. Injection is proved by recovering coordinates. It does not assert
   that an arbitrary multiplicity matrix with a zero column gives a faithful
   source multiplicity map; the factorStack source already has targetSize
   dimensions.

2. Nonunital channels and their supports. singleHom uses Pi.single, and each
   channel is a genuine nonunital star homomorphism into a common target.
   Its image of one is a projection. Different channels have orthogonal
   support units, which sum to the target unit. For a positive channel block
   size, an explicit supported diagonal entry equals one, so that block of
   the support is nonzero. Empty channels and zero-size blocks remain allowed.

3. Both squares and one common map. two_target_squares constructs the two
   target factor embeddings while sharing the same fixed commonAggregate.
   aggregate_realizations composes these with the actual earlier Realization
   maps. It proves both squares as literal equalities. All three channel maps
   are isometric by injection and the C*-norm theorem. Aggregate.left_unit and
   right_unit identify factor support units with the respective standard
   target inclusions of the very same common support unit. Factor projection,
   orthogonality and unit-sum statements are derived from those identities.

4. Integer lifting hypotheses are visible. integer_aggregate assumes actual
   positive integer diagrams and a nonempty target vertex type. The stronger
   simultaneous_aggregate assumes Nontrivial m (at least two target indices)
   and finite source/target indices, positive B and B', arbitrary actual
   integer-linear maps on their algebraic kernel/cokernel presentations, and
   arbitrary channel source weights. It invokes the proved simultaneous
   positive lifting with one common nonnegative determinant-one U. It does
   not assume realizations as a new unexplained existence hypothesis in that
   final result. Source index n may be empty; positivity can then be vacuous,
   and source algebras can be zero. This is a permitted degenerate instance,
   not a realization of nontrivial manuscript coefficient/K-theory data.

5. Support and weighted dimensions. positive_common_blocks requires a
   nonempty source index type, positive k in every channel, and every entry
   of T positive. Positive k alone supplies a positive contribution to both
   target halves, so no missing positivity hypothesis on l is needed for
   this sufficient condition. weighted_common_dimensions is an exact natural
   dimension identity for arbitrary weights q, including q=0. Scaling source
   sizes by q scales their dimension contribution by q while leaving the
   chosen channel multiplicity matrix T unchanged. With q=0 that channel has
   zero source and zero support. The nonzero-support lemma cannot be applied
   to it. The all-zero-weight and empty-channel cases compile independently.

6. Equal-source sums. When all source sizes agree, sumCommon/sumLeft/sumRight
   are actual unital star homomorphisms and both summed squares are proved.
   Their isometry theorem explicitly requires Nonempty C. This matters: an
   empty family has zero total target and cannot faithfully receive a general
   nonzero common source. No missing nonemptiness was found in that theorem.

Required boundaries and open interfaces
======================================
A. Aggregate (source lines 331–350) contains no HasMultiplicity field. Its
   parameter T enters target dimensions and the conditional support premise.
   aggregate_realizations uses the earlier multiplicity-controlled Realization
   internally, but only exports Nonempty Aggregate. The multiplicity witnesses
   are not retained for the resulting nonunital maps or their equal-source
   sums. The earlier HasMultiplicity predicate itself is for unital maps, so
   a supported-corner/nonunital extension or an equivalent concrete-coordinate
   certificate and composition theorem are needed here. Then induced K-theory
   functoriality must be proved separately. Do not treat simultaneous_aggregate
   as already proving that its arbitrary integer kernel/cokernel maps are the
   K-maps of the aggregate; the q=0 case makes that overinterpretation untenable.

B. The weighted dimension formula alone does not identify the finite diagram's
   universal full product with a matrix amplification of the original full
   product, or identify the associated tensor/coefficient maps. In manuscript
   section 5 the noise channel involves M_q(P); that bridge is still absent.

C. Nonzero support in each simple finite matrix block is established under
   the stated positive hypotheses. Ideal-theoretic fullness in the relevant
   amalgam/tensor/limit algebra, and the manuscript's evaluation/fullness
   arguments in later sections, are not formal consequences already supplied
   by this packet. No simplicity, nuclearity, pure infiniteness, KK-contractible
   coefficient model, graph-algebra identification, K-theory calculation, or
   ideal/fullness classification has been silently bundled into these sources.

D. Matrix/product CPAP is a permanence result conditional on coefficient CPAP.
   The exact target definition uses finite sets, every epsilon>0, positive
   matrix size, actual CP maps in both directions and norm contractions.
   Equivalence with other nuclearity definitions is still not proved here.

The packet is useful verified partial infrastructure within these limits.
Finding no blocking issue in this bounded audit does not establish full verification.
